D — Above the Fold
Goal identity — sphere pill and goal title — lives outside the card entirely, as a section heading above it. The card is purely functional: filter tabs and action items, nothing else. Two clear layers: "here's what we're working on" then "here's how." On Plan, the ring sidebar and the goal heading form a natural pair — both identify the goal, the card just executes it.

Plan G — Type-first
Flip the primary organization: group by what you need to do (Forward / Schedule / Find), with goals as labels within each group.
